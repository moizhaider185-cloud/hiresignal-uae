How matching works

This MVP uses exact normalized skill/role phrase matching, not AI. Match score: 60% skill overlap, 20% title relevance, 10% location preference, 10% experience eligibility. If advertised salary is below your minimum, the job is flagged separately. Scores are indicative and cannot predict hiring outcomes.

Never pay recruitment fees based on a listing alone. Independently verify employers and vacancies through official sources. Missing information is treated as unknown, not evidence of fraud.
